Beginners Guide to Volleyball

Learn the basics, build your skills, and enjoy the game!

Basic Rules

  • Six players per team on the court.
  • Each team can touch the ball up to three times.
  • Players cannot catch or hold the ball.
  • Score by landing the ball in the opponent's court.
  • Players rotate when their team wins the serve.

Volleyball Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Net
  • Court
  • Sports clothes
  • Volleyball shoes

Basic Techniques

  • Serving
  • Forearm passing (bumping)
  • Setting
  • Spiking
  • Blocking and digging

Quick Tip

Communicate with your teammates. Calling "Mine!!!!!" helps prevent confusion during a play.

Teamwork

Volleyball is a team sport. Support your teammates, stay ready, and keep trying.
